5 countries, 5 topics,
5 ways to move.

This Erasmus+ project took young people across Europe by train, on foot, by bike, by ferry and by campervan, and turned their stories into a living library. The trip is over. The stories keep going.

The concept

At Eurolibrary we bet on two things: a story can change a life, and a journey can unite a continent. So we travelled green across the EU to build a living library made of people, not books.

Five chapters, five ways to travel

Each mobility paired one green way of moving with one topic that matters to young Europeans. Here is what each chapter was about.

Bulgaria, by train

The Balkan Express

23 to 30 June 2025

More than 500 km by rail with young entrepreneurs and changemakers, who told their stories in four Human Libraries.

Spain, on foot

Buen Camino

26 July to 2 August 2025

Walking the English Way of the Camino de Santiago to talk about mental health, with four Human Libraries along the route.

Mental health Check the details
The Netherlands, by bike

The Dutch Way

1 to 8 September 2025

More than 150 km by bike across Friesland to share personal stories about healthy living in four public events.

Greece, by ferry

Waves of Hope

Three Cycladic islands by ferry, with Human Library events for local communities and school students, all around migration.

Green on Wheels
